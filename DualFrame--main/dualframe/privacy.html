Privacy Policy

How Dual Frame Media handles your information

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Dual Frame Media website (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you use the “Start a Project” form, we receive the details you type in:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your message, including anything you share about your project

If you contact us directly by email, phone, WhatsApp or social media, we also receive whatever details you share in that conversation.

Information stored on your device

If you use the light/dark mode switch, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers it on your next visit. It stays on your device, is not sent to us, and contains no personal information.

What we do not collect

We do not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts or tracking cookies on this website, and we do not ask for payment details.

2. How we use your information

  • To read and reply to your enquiry
  • To discuss, quote and deliver a project you ask us about
  • To keep records of our correspondence with you

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing emails unless you ask us to.

3. Who handles your information

To deliver form messages to our inbox, the contact form uses Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a form-handling service. Your form submission passes through their servers and is then emailed to us. Their handling of data is covered by their own privacy policy.

The pages you view also load resources from third parties, which may receive your IP address and browser details as part of normal web requests:

  • Google Fonts — to display the site’s typefaces
  • jsDelivr — to load the icon font

Links to Instagram, Behance, LinkedIn and WhatsApp take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for how they handle your data.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to respond and, if you become a client, for the length of the project and a reasonable time afterwards for our records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see section 6).

5. Security

The website and form submissions are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection when the site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive personal information through the form.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Delete your information

Email us at dualframemedia@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. You can also clear the saved theme preference at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

7. Children

This website is intended for businesses and creators and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
